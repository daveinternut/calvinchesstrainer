# Store listing text

The one source for the App Store and Google Play copy (English). Lead with the skills (board vision and notation), not the age group.

## Both stores

**Full description** (App Store "Description"; Play "Full description", up to 4000 characters):

```
Calvin Chess Trainer drills the skills that puzzles take for granted: seeing the whole board, and reading and finding squares and moves without thinking.

Daily warm-up
Five quick timed drills, about five minutes: find the checks, spot the loose pieces, find the forks, name squares from Black's side, then a mate in one.

Vision
• Find Checks: every check in a real game position
• Find Captures: every capture on the board
• Hanging Pieces: every undefended piece
• Forks & Skewers: the squares that hit two targets
• Knight Sight: every square a knight reaches
• Knight Flight: the fewest moves to the target
• Pawn Attack: slip past a wall of pawns
• Mate in 1: 400 positions, one move to mate

Notation
• Squares: find the square, fast, from White's side or Black's
• Files & Ranks: name every file and rank
• Read Moves: read a move, then play it
• Piece Letters: K, Q, R, B and N at a glance
• Piece Values: which side comes out ahead?

Opening Explorer
Play through the openings with book moves, Stockfish hints and an evaluation bar.

Practice your way
• Practice at your own pace, or race the clock in timed rounds
• Every drill remembers its setup, and Continue picks up where you left off
• Results show the answers you missed
• Personal bests, saved on your device
• Squares and pieces spoken aloud as you play
• Works offline

No ads, no in-app purchases and no account.

From Internut Education.
```
